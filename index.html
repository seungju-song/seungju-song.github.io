Game & System Design Level Balancing

I'm studying Computer Science (Real-Time Interactive Simulation) at DigiPen Institute of Technology. I came up through DigiPen's Game Engineering program at Keimyung University in Daegu, South Korea, and I'm now on DigiPen's Redmond campus.

I design before I code. In Dragonic Tactics, I drew per-class decision flowcharts before implementing any enemy AI; in Street Typer, I formed the team myself, wrote the GDD, and balanced the card system and difficulty. In a one-week game jam, I handled design, programming, art, and sound alone.

I also design systems beyond the usual combat/AI/card scope — UI layout, level progression, difficulty tuning — wherever the project needs it.

As a GAM150 TA, I helped students scope their projects down to something manageable. Before enrolling, I served two years in the Republic of Korea Air Force as an aircraft armament maintenance specialist.
